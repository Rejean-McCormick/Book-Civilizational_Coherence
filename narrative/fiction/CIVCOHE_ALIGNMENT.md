\[

\[
FictionalModuleName \neq CurrentImplementationStatus

## Relationship to the six parts of the nonfiction book

### Part I — Distributed Intelligence and Coherence

Most directly illuminated by F0003, F0009, F0016, F0017 and F0018: they make interfaces, whole-system legibility and the conversion from dispersed voices to organized capability narratively perceptible.

### Part II — Power, Incentives and System Dynamics

Most directly illuminated by F0001, F0002, F0010, F0011, F0014, F0015 and F0018: they dramatize capture, founder power, institutional opacity, revolt, accountability and non-domination.

### Part III — Knowledge, Memory and Meaning

Most directly illuminated by F0004–F0008, F0010, F0012, F0013, F0016–F0018: they explore plural contexts, language, symbolic mediation, memory, provenance and the separation of narrative truth from empirical claims.

### Part IV — From Knowledge to Collective Action

Most directly illuminated by F0006–F0009, F0014 and F0018: they stage judgment, attention, deliberation, translation of grievances into tasks/functions, and the passage from voice to organized action.

### Part V — Federation, Sovereignty and Resilience

Most directly illuminated by F0001, F0003, F0009–F0011 and F0018: they can be used to test independence, governance, anti-capture, replication, local autonomy and system-level resilience.

### Part VI — Cumulative Collective Agency

Most directly illuminated by F0009, F0010, F0016–F0018: they emphasize memory, transmission, mastery, criticism of the founder and the ability of an architecture or practice to survive its originator.

## Terminology bridge

Fiction preserves earlier names, speculative implementations and symbolic forms. Do not silently normalize the works themselves to match the current architecture.

| Narrative/design-history term | CivCohe handling rule |
|---|---|
| King Klown | narrative implementation of critic/trickster/founder-facing function; never mandatory infrastructure |
| KOA / kOA | preserve spelling and meaning in each source; do not infer that a fictional appearance defines the current architecture |
| Konnaxion | may be a character-facing or world-facing system name in fiction; current implementation claims come from architecture sources |
| Orgo / Ethikos / EkoH / SmartVote / KonnectED / keenKonnect / Kreative | treat fictional appearances as genealogy or functional imagination unless current architecture sources independently confirm status |
| Kristal Farms | may function as fictional/speculative infrastructure in narrative works; external-world feasibility/outcomes require technical and empirical sources |
| Pi / cosmic / religious symbolism | preserve as declared symbolic or fictional context; never convert into scientific or theological evidence |

## Authoring rule

When a narrative work is used in the nonfiction manuscript, identify the function of the use:

1. **illustration**;
2. **analogy**;
3. **stress-test**;
4. **mnemonic encoding**;
5. **design genealogy**;
6. **cultural precommitment**.

Then return to current architecture and evidence. No chapter should depend on accepting the fictional world as fact.
