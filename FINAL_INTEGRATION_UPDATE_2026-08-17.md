## Integrated concepts

### Relational centrality

\[
RelationalCentrality \neq SovereignCentrality
\]

\[
CenterOfCommunion \neq CenterOfDomination
\]

The hub may be central because it increases useful relations among parts, not because it commands them.

### Bounded Christ analogy

Christ may be used only as an **INTERPRETIVE ANALOGY** for themes such as sharing, service, communion and common belonging. The repository explicitly rejects use of that analogy as:

- theology;
- sacred legitimation;
- identification of the founder or King Klown with Christ;
- empirical evidence;
- compulsory symbolism.
