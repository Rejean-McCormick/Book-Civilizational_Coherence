# Unknown contributor enters a global reference corpus

## Situation
A 28-year-old independent contributor creates a high-quality physics Kristal. A recognized physicist reviews it and provides an initial attestation, opening a path to additional relevant review. In the future-institutional scenario, the contribution is eventually incorporated into a much larger reference aggregate while provenance preserves attribution.

## Coordination problem
Recognition is often path-dependent:

\[PriorStatus \rightarrow Visibility \rightarrow MoreStatus\]

The desired alternative is:

\[ArtifactQuality \rightarrow RelevantReview \rightarrow Attestation \rightarrow BroaderReview \rightarrow PossibleIncorporation\]

## Epistemic mobility
The mechanism is not universal democratization of truth. It is mobility into **relevant expert attention**.

One endorsement does not create scientific consensus.

## EkoH role
Help discover reviewers qualified for the domain and task while preserving:

\[Expertise \neq UniversalRank\]

## Kristal role
Carry structured claims, provenance, references, version history and validation context. If aggregated, attribution should remain traceable.

\[Aggregation \neq AttributionErasure\]

## KristALL / UNESCO status
`KristALL` is a conceptual name for a large crystallized knowledge aggregate. A UNESCO-run or UNESCO-stewarded instance is a **possible future institutional configuration**, not a current fact.

## Failure modes
- prestige cascade;
- closed review network;
- first-review bottleneck;
- EkoH becomes caste;
- endorsements become tradable status;
- disputed material appears as consensus;
- aggregation hides dissent history;
- provenance exists technically but is socially invisible.

## Potential tests
- submission-to-qualified-review latency;
- proportion receiving relevant review;
- relation between prior status and review access;
- provenance retention after aggregation;
- reviewer diversity;
- ability to contest validation.

## Historical analogy caution
Any manuscript link to Tim Berners-Lee or Semantic Web ideals must be separately sourced and historically verified.

## What this scenario does not prove
It does not prove UNESCO adoption, scientific-community acceptance, solved gatekeeping, or that provenance creates truth.
