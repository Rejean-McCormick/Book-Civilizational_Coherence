# Chapter 22 Brief — When Action Becomes Cumulative

**Status:** normalized V2 chapter contract. Higher authority than the detailed chapter specification for scope, canonical distinctions and claim status.

## Entry state
The architecture can act and survive; now evaluate what action leaves behind.

## Proof obligation
Define cumulative collective agency as action that preserves or increases reusable future capability, not merely present output.

## Main mechanism
Outcomes become gains only when learning is captured, reusable, maintained and integrated; capacity also depreciates.

## Canonical distinctions to protect
- Action ≠ Accumulation
- Success ≠ Cumulative Gain
- Failure ≠ Capacity Loss
- Output ≠ Outcome ≠ Learning ≠ Capability Gain
- Memory Growth ≠ Capability Growth

## Best worked example
Successful emergency response that burns out its team versus failed experiment that creates reusable knowledge and tooling.

## Serious counterexample / failure pressure
Cumulative capability can support harmful ends; growth is not goodness.

## kOA role
Cross-module feedback only as illustration of how outcomes could feed knowledge, tooling and training.

## Epistemic status
Future-capacity equation is conceptual; gain/depreciation require operational definitions.

## Reserved concepts / do not pre-solve
Human mastery reproduction and measurement.

## Exit condition
Even perfect artifacts are useless if nobody knows how to understand, maintain and evolve them.
