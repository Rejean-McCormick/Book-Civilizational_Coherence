# Chapter 24 Brief — Measuring Civilizational Coherence

**Status:** normalized V2 chapter contract. Higher authority than the detailed chapter specification for scope, canonical distinctions and claim status.

## Entry state
The book now has many mechanisms but no legitimate single scalar for coherence.

## Proof obligation
Define a measurement program using converging indicators across the conversion chain while resisting metric collapse.

## Main mechanism
Measure manifestations—discovery, quality, reuse, decision use, execution, outcomes, learning, resilience—and infer coherence as multidimensional/latent.

## Canonical distinctions to protect
- Measurement ≠ Definition
- Adoption ≠ Success
- Metric ≠ Goal
- Correlation ≠ Causation
- Single Score ≠ Coherence

## Best worked example
Adoption rises sharply while expert participation, reuse and outcomes remain flat.

## Serious counterexample / failure pressure
Metrics become targets; causal attribution is difficult; latency and quality trade off; privacy can limit measurement.

## kOA role
Use fork viability, offline continuity and credible recovery time only as test candidates.

## Epistemic status
Measurement framework is a research program, not a validated index.

## Reserved concepts / do not pre-solve
Ultimate failure/falsifiability criteria.

## Exit condition
Once claims are measurable, what observations would force us to narrow, revise or reject them?
