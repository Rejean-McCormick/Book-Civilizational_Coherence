# Chapter 17 Brief — Replication Before Federation

**Status:** normalized V2 chapter contract. Higher authority than the detailed chapter specification for scope, canonical distinctions and claim status.

## Entry state
A local institution can now perform the knowledge→action loop.

## Proof obligation
Argue that independent replication of a **complete coherent hub** should conceptually precede federation so strong local centrality does not require runtime dependence on the original center.

## Main mechanism
Instances begin from a common hub/kernel/specification, operate as strong centers at their own scale, then choose selective federation relationships.

## Canonical distinctions to protect
- Replication ≠ Federation
- Compatibility ≠ Terminal Dependency On Original Center
- Replication ≠ Rejection Of Centrality
- Polycentricity ≠ Centerlessness
- Copy ≠ Controlled Client
- Open Source ≠ Open Good

## Best worked example
Hospital or city instance installed locally and later federated without losing local operation.

## Serious counterexample / failure pressure
Common origin does not guarantee long-term compatibility; replicas can drift or remain operationally dependent on founder tooling.

## kOA role
Replicable instances, open-good package, local core; USB/local tests. Treat implementation state carefully.

## Epistemic status
Replication-before-federation is an accepted design principle; actual independence must be verified.

## Reserved concepts / do not pre-solve
Divergence, scale and permeability.

## Exit condition
Once independent replicas exist, they will diverge. When does divergence become evolution rather than fragmentation?
