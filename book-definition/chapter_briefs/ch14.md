# Chapter 14 Brief — Attention as a Coordination Resource

**Status:** normalized V2 chapter contract. Higher authority than the detailed chapter specification for scope, canonical distinctions and claim status.

## Entry state
Chapter 13 created a judgment landscape richer than any one human can inspect.

## Proof obligation
Treat attention as a scarce coordination resource and distinguish routing from universal ranking.

## Main mechanism
Signals compete for limited cognitive/institutional processing; relevance is relational to actor, task, context and time.

## Canonical distinctions to protect
- Information ≠ Attention
- Visibility ≠ Relevance
- Popularity ≠ Importance
- Urgency ≠ Importance
- Routing ≠ Global Ranking
- Demand For Investigation ≠ Evidence For Claim
- Salience ≠ Evidence

## Best worked example
Unknown high-quality warning versus celebrity intervention; slow risk versus viral controversy.

## Serious counterexample / failure pressure
Contextual routing can reproduce expert lock-in; metrics can be gamed; personalization can hide common reality.

## kOA role
Konnaxion/EkoH routing, Smart Vote divergence as signal, ethiKos agenda formation. Narrative salience can trigger investigation but not settle truth.

## Epistemic status
Attention-priority formulas are heuristics; value requires behavioral evaluation.

## Reserved concepts / do not pre-solve
Authority to decide.

## Exit condition
Once good advice reaches the right people, who legitimately has the right to decide?
