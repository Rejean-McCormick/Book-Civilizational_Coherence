# Chapter 2 Brief — Civilizational Coherence

**Status:** normalized V2 chapter contract. Higher authority than the detailed chapter specification for scope, canonical distinctions and claim status.

## Entry state
Chapter 1 established potential without agency.

## Proof obligation
Define civilizational coherence as a dynamic capacity of functional composition and distinguish it from its tempting false equivalents.

## Main mechanism
Coherence rises when relevant capacities become discoverable, intelligible and composable while correction, pluralism and autonomy remain possible. A strong articulation hub can increase coherence when it centralizes access/composition without monopolizing truth, sovereignty or continuation.

## Canonical distinctions to protect
- Coherence ≠ Consensus
- Coherence ≠ Decentralization
- Functional Centrality ≠ Domination
- Central Hub ≠ Centralized Sovereignty
- Coherence ≠ Maximum Connectivity
- Coherence ≠ Homogeneity
- Coherence ≠ Goodness
- Structural Coherence ≠ Legible Coherence

## Best worked example
Revisit the agricultural crisis with the same human potential but different interface quality.

## Serious counterexample / failure pressure
A highly coherent system can amplify error or oppression. Correction and non-domination are necessary constraints, not automatic consequences.

## kOA role
Minimal: kOA as an attempt to compose capabilities without requiring consensus or absorption.

## Epistemic status
Civilizational coherence is a conceptual construct/research program, not a measured scalar.

## Reserved concepts / do not pre-solve
Detailed loss families, interface mechanisms, capture and metrics.

## Exit condition
If coherence is the missing property, where exactly does capacity disappear when coherence is weak?
