# Continuity and Dependency Map

The 25 chapters are a progressive derivation of one problem, not 25 topics.

## Part I — capacity and conversion
1. Distributed capacity exists.
2. Coherence names the missing capacity of composition.
3. Losses explain where capacity disappears.
4. Interfaces locate the transitions where losses occur.

## Part II — why bad interfaces persist
5. Systems reproduce their own conditions.
6. Competition/collaboration are infrastructural regimes.
7. Expertise and credibility can be structured without equating status with competence.
8. Coordination infrastructure creates power, capture risks and need for counterpowers.

## Part III — how knowledge survives
9. Experience becomes cumulative only through memory/reuse.
10. Knowledge must survive the platform that produced it.
11. Portability creates plural branches; pluralism must preserve unequal evidence.
12. Plural knowledge must remain semantically intelligible across languages/institutions.

## Part IV — from knowledge to action
13. Comparable contributions still require collective readings.
14. Useful readings compete for scarce attention.
15. Expert advice still does not decide.
16. Legitimate decisions still do not execute themselves.

## Part V — composition without absorption
17. A strong coherent hub should be independently replicable as a whole before optional federation.
18. Replicated hubs may diverge; sovereignty requires lineage, bridges and the ability to evolve without erasing common origin.
19. Information, authority and execution can live at different scales.
20. Boundaries must permit selective exchange rather than total openness/isolation.
21. Local canonical operation should survive loss of network or AI where those dependencies are not intrinsic.

## Part VI — closing the cumulative loop
22. Action becomes cumulative only when it leaves reusable capability.
23. Artifacts are insufficient if human mastery is not reproduced.
24. Coherence must be inferred from multiple indicators, not a magic score.
25. Theory and architecture must expose themselves to failure, revision and replacement.

## Conclusion
The deepest cumulative capacity is the ability to improve the process of knowing, coordinating, acting, correcting and transmitting itself.

## Transition logic

1 → 2: If intelligence does not automatically become agency, what property makes it composable?  
2 → 3: If coherence is the property, where does capacity disappear when coherence is low?  
3 → 4: Losses cluster at transitions; study interfaces.  
4 → 5: If interfaces can be redesigned, why do inefficient ones persist?  
5 → 6: What infrastructural regimes reproduce competition/collaboration?  
6 → 7: If collaboration matters, who receives epistemic attention on which questions?  
7 → 8: Whoever determines competence, credibility or visibility exercises power.  
8 → 9: Power/accountability depend on what the system remembers.  
9 → 10: Memory trapped in one platform remains fragile.  
10 → 11: Portable knowledge can fork; how can pluralism avoid relativism?  
11 → 12: Before comparing claims, do actors mean the same thing?  
12 → 13: Once contributions are comparable, how do we synthesize without forced uniformity?  
13 → 14: Once many readings exist, which deserve attention?  
14 → 15: Once advice reaches a legitimate authority, who decides?  
15 → 16: Even a good decision can fail in execution.  
16 → 17: Once an architecture works locally, how can it spread without a new center?  
17 → 18: Independent replicas can diverge; how does divergence become evolution rather than fragmentation?  
18 → 19: Branches/institutions operate at different scales; how should functions be placed?  
19 → 20: What should cross institutional boundaries?  
20 → 21: Can the local core survive network/AI absence?  
21 → 22: A resilient system can act; does action increase future capacity?  
22 → 23: Reusable artifacts still require people who can understand/maintain them.  
23 → 24: If capability is cumulative, how do we measure it?  
24 → 25: Measurement creates genuine failure criteria.  
25 → Conclusion: A system that learns from failure can improve its capacity to improve.

## Spiral rule

When a thread returns:
- state what was already established;
- add the new scale/mechanism;
- avoid re-proving the old claim.
