# kOA Implementation Boundaries and Architecture Map

## Role in the book

kOA is the recurring **architectural laboratory**.

It is not:
- proof of theory;
- the only compatible architecture;
- a sovereign solution for civilization;
- a reason to introduce a design requirement before deriving it.

Canonical line:

> **kOA is used as an architectural laboratory and concrete case, not as the premise of the theory.**

## Stable function → current ecosystem concept

| General function | Current concept |
|---|---|
| broad coordination/governance/intelligence fabric | Konnaxion |
| contextual expertise/credibility evidence/readings | EkoH |
| parallel reproducible readings of participation | Smart Vote |
| deliberation/governance substrate | ethiKos |
| decision-to-execution coordination | Orgo |
| portable structured knowledge artifact | Kristal |
| semantic concepts/relations/mappings | SemantiK |
| ambiguity/uncertainty preservation | SenTient |
| deterministic language realization | SemantiK Architect |
| education/mastery reproduction | UCKK |
| higher ecosystem grouping | Koali |

King Klown is **not** a mandatory technical component. It belongs to a narrative/publishing/UCKK context and can be removed or rethemed.

## Konnaxion

Konnaxion is the broader coordination/governance/intelligence fabric.

It may include/connect functions for:
- education and expertise discovery;
- deliberation;
- collective readings;
- project/organizational coordination;
- governance.

Do not describe Konnaxion as the knowledge artifact itself.

## Kristal

Canonical distinction:

\[
Kristal \neq Konnaxion
\]

Konnaxion is mechanics/coordination; Kristal is content/knowledge artifact.

A Kristal should be conceptually usable:
- without Konnaxion;
- in another software system;
- offline;
- as a forkable/versioned artifact.

It can represent:
- scientific reference;
- minority hypothesis;
- religion;
- fiction;
- fantasy worlds.

\[
KristalExists \neq KristalIsTrue
\]

## EkoH

Do not describe EkoH as a universal reputation score.

Competence is contextual:
- domain;
- task;
- context;
- time.

Evidence may include:
- formal credentials;
- patents;
- publications;
- demonstrated skill;
- successful predictions;
- elected/institutional experience;
- peer attestations;
- internal performance.

Principle:

\[
Evidence \rightarrow ContextualCompetenceEstimate
\]

not:

\[
Prestige \rightarrow UniversalAuthority
\]

Underlying evidence should be more portable and durable than derived scores.

## Smart Vote

Smart Vote produces multiple lenses/readings over canonical participation signals.

Raw/canonical data remain distinct from readings.

No single lens is sovereign.

Suggested/default lenses create attention power and should be:
- transparent;
- versioned;
- contestable;
- open to alternatives.

Smart Vote output is not automatically a sovereign political decision.

## ethiKos

ethiKos is a deliberative/governance substrate, especially for subjective, ethical and political questions.

It can structure:
- questions;
- arguments;
- evidence;
- positions;
- participation;
- governance processes.

Do not portray it as universal moral authority.

## Orgo

Orgo addresses:

\[
Decision \not\Rightarrow Execution
\]

It can coordinate:
- ownership;
- tasks;
- dependencies;
- milestones;
- escalation;
- feedback.

Action does not have to transit through Orgo. Existing institutions may execute through their own systems.

## UCKK

UCKK is an educational/publishing/learning institution built on the architecture, not a universal governance layer.

Book-level role:
- reproduce human mastery;
- teach maintenance/evolution;
- create paths from user to contributor to maintainer/bridge-builder.

UCKK can be duplicated and rethemed. King Klown can disappear or become a generic critic/trickster/journalist/catalyst.

## SemantiK / SenTient / SemantiK Architect

### SemantiK
Structures concepts, relations, definitions and mappings.

### SenTient
Preserves ambiguity, uncertainty and unresolved interpretations.

### SemantiK Architect
Performs deterministic language realization from structured meaning using language-specific resources.

Canonical chain:

\[
Meaning
\rightarrow
StructuredRepresentation
\rightarrow
LanguageSpecificRealization
\]

## AI stance

Current book-level architecture:

\[
AI\text{-}assisted\ construction
\rightarrow
ValidatedDeterministicArtifact
\rightarrow
AI\text{-}independent\ operation
\]

Canonical runtime should not require AI where the function is intended to be deterministic.

AI may be used:
- to build;
- test;
- generate reference material;
- assist external analysis;
- propose interpretations.

Older documents may contain hybrid/AI-runtime descriptions. Treat these as earlier iterations unless current implementation evidence says otherwise.

## Instances

Instances may exist at many scales:
- individual;
- team;
- school;
- hospital;
- company;
- association;
- municipality;
- national;
- global.

They may be public, restricted, internal or offline/hermetic.

Scale does not determine openness, connectivity or governance.

## Replication and federation

Instances begin compatible because of common origin/kernel, not central runtime dependence.

Federation is optional.

Local operation should survive federation-link failure for functions not intrinsically dependent on federation.

## Open good

The public-good aspiration is stronger than open source.

An **open good** should make enough of the functional good portable—code, specifications, knowledge, configuration, documentation and operational capability—to support independent use/substitution where rights allow.

## Capture

Do not claim capture is impossible.

Canonical claim:

> kOA aims to make capture non-terminal, visible, contestable and bypassable.

\[
InstanceCapture \neq SystemCapture
\]

when alternatives remain operational and credible.

Technical fork time may be short; social legitimacy and migration can dominate recovery.

## Credibility registry

This remains a hard design problem.

Prefer:
- tamper-evident evidence/attestations;
- portable evidence;
- contextual local computation;

over immutable universal human score.

Design intuition:

\[
StableEvidence + MutableInterpretation
\]

## Privacy

Pseudonymity is not anonymity.

Different contexts may require:
- nominative participation;
- pseudonymous participation;
- anonymous participation with eligibility proof;
- aggregate results.

Audit power without automatically exposing every person.

## Seeded simulations

AI-generated simulations based on public sources may help avoid an empty start.

Labels must distinguish:
- verbatim source;
- structured paraphrase;
- synthetic simulation;
- content validated by the real person.

Never let synthetic simulation appear as authentic statement.

## Institutional stewardship

Possible global stewards such as UNESCO or domain institutions are configurations/hypotheses, not permanent exclusive authorities.

Leadership can be:
- domain-relative;
- polycentric;
- replaceable.

## Implementation-state rule

When writing:
- do not assert current implementation from memory;
- distinguish intended/final architecture from verified state;
- verify current technical docs when actual implementation matters.

The theory should outlive modules and names.


## V2 additions — project-specific constraints and experimental mechanisms

### Original public-good non-monetization constraint

The original public-good UCKK/Koali-oriented reference platform is intended not to be monetized as the public good itself.

**Status:** project-specific normative/institutional constraint.

This is not a theorem that all downstream instances or organizations must use one economic model.

### Latent competence discovery experiment

One proposed experiment is to expose some low-recognition/general participants to selected questions before established experts, then compare repeated predictions with later expert reference judgments and, where available, outcomes.

**Status:** EXPERIMENTAL PROPOSAL.

Safeguards:
- agreement with experts is not truth;
- repeated tasks matter more than one lucky answer;
- compare with outcomes where possible;
- avoid defining competence circularly as agreement with incumbents;
- do not turn this one signal into a global rank.

### Seeded simulation succession

When synthetic simulations represent public figures or institutions:
- provenance remains visible;
- the real person may authenticate/correct/take control under the relevant rules;
- later changes of position should be represented as changes, not silent replacement of history.

### De facto standards

A specialized branch may become a de facto standard if other instances voluntarily adopt it because it performs well.

**Status:** systemic hypothesis/design possibility, not guaranteed convergence.

### Credibility infrastructure remains unresolved

Tamper-evident attestations and portable evidence are the preferred direction, but the exact technical registry mechanism remains unresolved.

Blockchain-like approaches may be explored but are not canonical.
