## UCKK intent vs legal encoding

Current project intent:

> People are encouraged to duplicate the UCKK institutional model, code and training architecture, replace King Klown or other symbolic roles, and build their own personal, collective, corporate or disciplinary channels.

This register deliberately does **not** claim that every current file already carries a license that perfectly implements that intent.

The legal documents should eventually be made congruent with the intended open-good model.
