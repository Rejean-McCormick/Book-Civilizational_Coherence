# Chapter 19 Brief — Multi-Scale Institutions

**Status:** normalized V2 chapter contract. Higher authority than the detailed chapter specification for scope, canonical distinctions and claim status.

## Entry state
Replication and divergence create many sovereign instances.

## Proof obligation
Show that information, authority and execution can legitimately live at different scales and require cross-scale interfaces rather than one hierarchy.

## Main mechanism
Functional scope, externalities, local knowledge and capacity influence appropriate scale; polycentric institutions coordinate horizontally and vertically.

## Canonical distinctions to protect
- Scale ≠ Openness
- Scale ≠ Connectivity
- Scale ≠ Governance
- Scale of Information ≠ Scale of Authority ≠ Scale of Execution
- Local ≠ Automatically Better

## Best worked example
Hospital keeps detailed clinical data local while sharing validated epidemic aggregates with public health.

## Serious counterexample / failure pressure
Subsidiarity can become excuse for under-capacity localism; central coordination can be necessary for externalities/common infrastructure.

## kOA role
Same functional grammar across local/domain/global instances; UCKK remains an educational case, not universal model.

## Epistemic status
Functional subsidiarity is a design principle; appropriate scale is empirical/contextual.

## Reserved concepts / do not pre-solve
What crosses boundaries.

## Exit condition
Once scales interact, which data, knowledge and actions should actually cross?
