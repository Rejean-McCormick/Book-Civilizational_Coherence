# Chapter 20 Brief — Selective Permeability

**Status:** normalized V2 chapter contract. Higher authority than the detailed chapter specification for scope, canonical distinctions and claim status.

## Entry state
Multi-scale institutions need exchange without total exposure.

## Proof obligation
Define selective permeability as boundary control preserving useful composition, privacy, autonomy and failure containment.

## Main mechanism
Relations are explicit by type, scope, version, direction and permission; selected artifacts/aggregates cross while other state remains local.

## Canonical distinctions to protect
- Connection ≠ Total Exposure
- Portable ≠ Public
- Exit ≠ Total Disconnection
- Selective Permeability ≠ Isolation

## Best worked example
Hospitals share epidemiological aggregates/Kristals but not full patient records.

## Serious counterexample / failure pressure
Too much permeability expands blast radius; too little recreates silos. Access controls can themselves become capture points.

## kOA role
Scoped federation contracts, partial federation, local data gravity and branch-specific relationships.

## Epistemic status
Selective permeability is accepted design principle; exact boundaries are domain-specific.

## Reserved concepts / do not pre-solve
Determinism/offline/AI dependency.

## Exit condition
If local sovereignty is real, can the core still operate when network or AI services disappear?
