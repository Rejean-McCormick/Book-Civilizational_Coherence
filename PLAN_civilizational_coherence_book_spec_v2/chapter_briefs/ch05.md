# Chapter 5 Brief — Why Systems Reproduce Themselves

**Status:** normalized V2 chapter contract. Higher authority than the detailed chapter specification for scope, canonical distinctions and claim status.

## Entry state
The first four chapters make the problem look partly engineerable.

## Proof obligation
Explain why systems can reproduce inefficient or incoherent structures without requiring conspiracy.

## Main mechanism
Structure shapes behavior; behavior produces outcomes; outcomes reinforce or modify structure through feedback, incentives, expectations and path dependence.

## Canonical distinctions to protect
- Persistence ≠ Optimality
- Values ≠ Incentives
- Historical Origin ≠ Current Reproduction Mechanism
- Metric ≠ Goal

## Best worked example
A credential/status loop or bureaucratic metric that reproduces itself despite local dissatisfaction.

## Serious counterexample / failure pressure
Persistence can reflect genuine function or constraint, not merely pathology.

## kOA role
Minimal: durable redesign must alter reproduction conditions, not only interfaces on paper.

## Epistemic status
Systems-dynamics mechanisms require literature/examples; avoid mystical attractor language.

## Reserved concepts / do not pre-solve
Competition/collaboration regime, expertise and capture.

## Exit condition
Which infrastructures make competition or collaboration self-reproducing regimes?
