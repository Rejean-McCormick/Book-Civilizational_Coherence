# Canonical Formula and Relation Register

The book should not become equation-heavy. This register separates **canonical recurrent relations** from local heuristics.

## 1. Core chain

\[
DistributedPotential
\rightarrow
Coherence
\rightarrow
CollectiveAgency
\rightarrow
Learning
\rightarrow
FutureCapacity
\]

**Literal translation:** distributed potential becomes collective agency through coherence; action can generate learning that affects future capacity.

**Meaning:** the book studies transformations between these states.

**Status:** conceptual architecture of the book.

## 2. Agency and losses

\[
A_t=P_t-L_t
\]

**Literal translation:** effective agency at time \(t\) is represented as available potential minus losses preventing mobilization.

**Meaning:** capacity can increase through new potential or lower avoidable loss.

**Status:** canonical conceptual model; not calibrated subtraction of commensurable quantities.

## 3. Conversion chain

\[
Potential
\rightarrow
Discovery
\rightarrow
Interpretation
\rightarrow
Selection
\rightarrow
Coordination
\rightarrow
Execution
\rightarrow
Evaluation
\rightarrow

\]

**Meaning:** each arrow is a possible loss interface.

**Status:** recurrent process model.

## 4. Reproduction loop

\[
Structure_t
\rightarrow
Behavior_t
\rightarrow
Outcome_t
\rightarrow
Structure_{t+1}
\]

**Meaning:** outcomes alter conditions generating future behavior.

**Status:** systems-dynamics conceptual loop.

## 5. Evidence and interpretation

\[
StableEvidence + RevisableInterpretation
\]

**Meaning:** preserve underlying evidence/attestations more strongly than derived rankings.

**Status:** design principle.

## 6. Counterpowers

\[
Verification + Contestation + Substitution + Exit
\]

**Meaning:** domination is constrained by combined ability to inspect, challenge, replace and leave.

**Status:** normative/design framework.

## 7. Cumulative knowledge

\[
Experience
\rightarrow
Capture
\rightarrow
Interpretation
\rightarrow
Codification
\rightarrow
Transmission
\rightarrow
Reuse
\]

**Meaning:** experience becomes cumulative only if it survives enough transformations to be reusable.

**Status:** conceptual process model.

## 8. Semantic realization

\[
Meaning
\rightarrow
StructuredRepresentation
\rightarrow
LanguageSpecificRealization
\]

**Meaning:** language output can be generated from structured meaning without making one natural language the permanent semantic truth layer.

**Status:** conceptual/architectural relation.

## 9. Facts and readings

\[
Facts \neq Readings
\]

and:

\[
OneParticipationRecord
\rightarrow
MultipleReadings
\]

**Meaning:** derived judgments are interpretations of a common record, not replacements for it.

**Status:** canonical architectural distinction.

## 10. Advice to outcome

\[
Evidence
\rightarrow
Advice
\rightarrow
Decision
\rightarrow
Action
\rightarrow
Outcome
\]

**Meaning:** epistemic advice and legitimate authority are connected but distinct; execution and outcomes remain separate.

**Status:** conceptual chain.

## 11. Future capacity

\[
\mathbf P_{t+1}
=
\mathbf P_t
+
\mathbf G_t
-
\mathbf D_t
\]

**Literal translation:** future capability equals present capability plus durable gains minus degradation.

**Meaning:** action is cumulative only to the extent it creates reusable capacity and limits depreciation.

**Status:** conceptual model; vector notation signals multidimensional capacity.

## 12. Falsifiability loop

\[
Claim
\rightarrow
ExpectedObservation
\rightarrow
Test
\rightarrow
PossibleFailure
\rightarrow
RevisionOrRejection
\]

**Meaning:** major hypotheses expose themselves to observations that can weaken or reject them.

**Status:** epistemic discipline.

## Local heuristics

The detailed chapter specifications contain many useful local relations:
- effective-potential heuristics;
- multiplicative loss heuristics;
- contextual competence \(E(u,d,t,c)\);
- \(T_{credible\ recovery}\);
- attention-priority heuristics;
- lens functions;
- semantic-readiness metrics.

Use only if:
1. it adds explanatory structure;
2. variables are meaningful;
3. prose is not clearer;
4. status is explicit;
5. it will not be mistaken for calibrated science.
