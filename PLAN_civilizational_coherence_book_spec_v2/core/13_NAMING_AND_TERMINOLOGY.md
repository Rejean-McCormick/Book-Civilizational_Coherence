## Functional naming rule

Do not introduce proper name before function.

Example:
> "a portable, versioned knowledge artifact—called a Kristal in the concrete architecture"

rather than:
> "Kristal solves..."

## King Klown

Narrative role/character, not technical module in Koali.

Institutional UCKK iterations can remove or retheme it.

Do not imply mandatory adoption.

## Open good

Use **open good** for broader concept:
- source;
- useful artifacts;
- specifications;
- configuration;
- documentation;
- portable functional capability.

Do not treat as legal term without legal analysis.

## Agency

Collective agency is operational:
capacity of multiple actors to produce coordinated effects.

No literal collective consciousness claim.

## Civilization

"civilizational" = scale of distributed capacities/institutions, not homogeneous culture or ranking of civilizations.

## Expertise

Prefer:
- contextual competence;
- relevant expertise;
- evidence of competence.

Avoid global human "merit" score.

## Ethics/integrity

Prefer:
- procedural integrity;
- verified violation;
- contextual consequence.

Avoid universal moral rating.

## Canonical

"Canonical" means reference state/version/output under stated rules.

\[
Canonical \neq Mandatory \neq Permanent
\]

## Raw data

"Raw" often means underlying canonical record before a reading, not necessarily public/unprocessed/free of privacy restrictions.

## Deterministic

Same canonical input/rules/version → same canonical output.

Does not mean correct or unbiased.

## AI

Distinguish:
- AI used to build/test;
- optional AI used externally;
- AI required for canonical core.

Current intended architecture favors first two, rejects third where deterministic operation is intended.

## Narrative terms

Use **legible coherence** for general property.

Use **narrative mediation** for broader mechanism.

Use **The Surreal** for the project's particular method.

This keeps theory independent of one artistic implementation.
